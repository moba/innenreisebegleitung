# Privacy Policy

*This is a translation provided for convenience. The [German version]({{< ref path="datenschutz.md" lang="de" >}}) is the legally binding one.*

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is [Moritz Bartl, Munich]({{< ref "kontakt.md" >}}).

## Visiting this website

This website uses no cookies, no reach measurement, no analytics or tracking services, no externally loaded fonts and no embedded third-party content.

When the site is accessed, my hosting provider (Serverprofis GmbH, Feldkirchen near Munich) automatically logs access data (IP address, date and time, page requested, volume of data transferred, status code, referrer, browser and operating system identifier). The purpose is the technically faultless provision and the security of the website. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in the secure operation of the site. The logs are deleted after seven days at the latest. The hosting provider processes the data as a processor pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR; the servers are located in Germany.

## Contacting me

If you contact me by telephone, email, Signal or post, I process the information you provide in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for enquiries concerning possible collaboration, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. If you voluntarily share information about your health with me, I process it on the basis of your consent pursuant to Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. I delete enquiries as soon as they have been dealt with and no statutory retention periods apply.

A note on confidentiality: email and telephone are not end-to-end encrypted. For confidential content, please use Signal.

Service provider for telephone and voicemail: Fonial GmbH, Cologne.

Linked services: Signal and LinkedIn are included as ordinary links; no data is transmitted unless you click the links. If you use these services, the privacy policies of the respective providers apply, and they are independently responsible in that respect.

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20), as well as the right to withdraw consent once given at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Under Art. 21 GDPR you have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Irrespective of this, you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, Promenade 27, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

Further information on the processing of data in the context of an accompaniment will be provided separately before it begins.
